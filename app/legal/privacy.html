4. Who else handles it

  • Meta (Instagram) and Google (YouTube): where your comment lives; we read it and reply through their APIs.
  • Our hosting provider: runs the server that makes the videos and stores the records below.
  • ElevenLabs: turns the quiz host's lines into speech, which can include a winner's username (story narrators never say names).
  • Anthropic: the AI that writes each week's quizzes reads that week's theme suggestions (username and comment) to choose one; the AI that plans story videos only sees vote totals.
  • Discord: our private channel for previews and posting notes, which can name quiz shout-out winners (story video notes have totals only).

We don't share data with anyone else, and never sell it. Some of these providers are in the USA. Transfers rely on the UK/EU adequacy arrangements for the US (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension) where the provider is certified, or on the standard contractual clauses / UK International Data Transfer Addendum in their terms.

6. Security

The data sits on a private server reachable only over HTTPS; the owner's dashboard and controls need a secret token or sign-in; platform logins are stored with restricted file permissions; we collect as little as we can and remove names on a schedule.

7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR you can ask to access what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, or object to our use of it (then we stop for good). Portability doesn't apply to data used under legitimate interests, but we'll send you a copy on request. It's free, and we answer within one month.

How to opt out or have your data deleted

  1. Comment #optout under any of our videos, on any of our accounts. The bot picks it up within 12 hours and you're opted out (the platform confirms it was you).
  2. Use the opt-out and deletion form: it takes effect immediately and gives you a confirmation code.
  3. Email $mail with your username and platform.

One request covers every account we run, including any we add later: whichever account you ask on, your comments stop being used on all of them. Opting out means we never read, show, credit or reply to you again, your votes aren't counted and your bets aren't taken. Deleting also removes you from every record we keep. If you want a published video that shows your name taken down or edited, say so and we'll do it. You can check any request at $base/privacy/status with your confirmation code. If you removed our app from your Instagram or Facebook account, Meta tells us automatically and we delete what we hold.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority in the EU, though we'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.
