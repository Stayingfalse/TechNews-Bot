How to opt out or have your data deleted

One opt-out covers every account we run. There's no opting out of one account but not another: whichever account you ask on, you're opted out of the quiz, the dungeon crawl, the races, Farside, and any account we add later.

  1. Comment #optout under any of our videos, on any of our accounts. The bot picks it up within 12 hours and you're opted out (the platform confirms it was you).
  2. Use the opt-out and deletion form: it gives you a short code. Comment the code under any of our videos from the account you named, and the request is carried out within minutes. This checks the request really comes from you, so nobody can delete someone else's data (such as a racing balance). Can't comment from that account? Email us with the code.
  3. Email $mail with your username and platform.

Opting out means that, on every account, we never read, show, credit or reply to you again: your quiz answers aren't checked, your story votes aren't counted and you leave the party, your bets aren't taken, and your settler leaves Farside. Deleting also removes you from every record we keep, on every account, including your racing balance and bets. If you want a published video that shows your name taken down or edited, say so and we'll do it. You can check any request at $base/privacy/status with your confirmation code. If you removed our app from your Instagram or Facebook account, Meta tells us automatically and we delete what we hold.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority in the EU, though we'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.
