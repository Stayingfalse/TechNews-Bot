social video. Each was checked on its OpenGameArt page, which lists CC0 among its licences (where a page
offers several, we use it under CC0).

Changes: re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis (q2, stereo, 44.1 kHz), metadata stripped, and anything past 95 s cut
with a 2 s fade (a quiz is at most ~90 s and the music always starts from the top).
